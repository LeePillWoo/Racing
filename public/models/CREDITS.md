# 3D asset credits

`cars.glb` and `trackside.glb` are built from the models below, downloaded from
[Fab](https://www.fab.com) and used under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Every model was modified for this game.

## Cars — `cars.glb`

Each car was cut into a body, a front piece, a rear piece and a single wheel, stretched to fit the
game's chassis, and stripped of its textures and materials; the game paints every surface itself.
